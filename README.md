## Projects

**PitStop** — [live demo](https://pitstop-p8k0.onrender.com/app) *(hosted on Render's free tier — may take ~30s to wake)*
A web app that helps Toronto rideshare drivers find the 3 nearest open washrooms from their GPS location. FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL, Docker, Leaflet.js map, 877 real locations merged from OpenStreetMap and Toronto Open Data. Built from a problem I had during ~20 months of driving rideshare — and used daily on real shifts.

**Toronto Road Intel**
A computer-vision pipeline that detects and maps road damage from dashcam footage. Collected my own driving data, synced it with GPS, trained and ran YOLOv8 inference, and mapped results with Folium. Python, OpenCV, SQLite.

**JobFlow** — AI job-search assistant
Parses job postings and resumes, then runs a multi-step pipeline to highlight skill gaps and suggest improvements. FastAPI, Pydantic, structured outputs.

**AI Code Review Tool**
